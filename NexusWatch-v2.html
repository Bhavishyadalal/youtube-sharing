A little help, if you need it.

01 / Invite your person

Create a room and share the link. Keep the host’s tab open. Only one friend can connect at a time.

02 / Set the mood

Paste a YouTube link or video ID. Both people can play, pause, seek, and edit the queue. “Play together” schedules a shared countdown.

03 / Make yourself heard

Start a voice call and wait for your friend to accept. Microphones start muted. Push-to-talk works with the hold button or Space when focus is not on a control.

Mobile playback

Each person may need to tap the YouTube player once. Background tabs, ads, live streams, and buffering can affect synchronization.

Keyboard shortcuts

Space: play/pause · ←/→: seek 10 seconds · F: fullscreen · M: local video mute · Escape: close dialog or fullscreen.
